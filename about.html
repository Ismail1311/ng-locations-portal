Our Mission

We build tools for Nigerian developers, by Nigerian developers.

For years, building apps for the Nigerian market meant dealing with the same frustrating problem: manually copying, pasting, and maintaining massive lists of states and Local Government Areas. It was a waste of time and prone to errors.

We created the NG Locations API to solve this. We believe that infrastructure data should be accessible, accurate, and incredibly easy to integrate.

Our API handles the data, the updates, and the security so you can focus on building what actually matters: your product.
