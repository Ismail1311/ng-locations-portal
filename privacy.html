Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2024

At NG Locations API, your privacy is a top priority. This policy explains how we handle your data.

1. Data We Collect

We collect your email address and username when you register. We also securely store SHA-256 hashes of your API keys. We never store your raw API keys on our servers.

2. How We Use Data

We use your email strictly for authentication and critical account alerts. We do not sell your data to third parties. We do not track your API usage for advertising purposes.

3. Security

All data is transmitted over HTTPS. Your API keys are hashed, meaning even if our database was compromised, your keys would remain safe.
